## 5. What is still uncertain?

- Generated token counts and EOS presence are not recorded in the preserved artifacts; the decoder length cap is inferred from the runtime-ceiling signature and is not confirmed by token-level evidence.
- Script-related mismatch counts use an independent difflib word alignment and are lower-bound estimates; they can differ from the strict scorer's alignment and never replace the strict metric.
- The repetition heuristics are content heuristics. A clip is not a confirmed hallucination solely because a heuristic triggered.
- Attribution WER values that exclude failure classes are reported for diagnosis only; the corpus WER of record remains the strict metric.
- Loop onset time within the audio is unknown; the artifacts contain no timing information beyond total runtime.

## 7. How will we evaluate whether selection helped?

- Comparison: identical-budget random versus intelligent selection, same base checkpoint and revision, same hyperparameters and step budget, matched example count and total audio duration
- Evaluation set: the frozen 47-speaker held-out reserve from mendspeech.mucs_holdout_pool.v0; never used for tuning
- Primary metrics: corpus WER (strict mendspeech.hinglish.v1); macro utterance WER; insertions and generation-cap hit share; code-switched versus Hindi-dominant bucket WER
- Success criterion: intelligent arm improves corpus WER over the random arm by at least 10% relative at identical budget, before any aggregate claim per-difficulty-slice results must be reported
- Policy: `docs/hinglish_evaluation_policy.md`
